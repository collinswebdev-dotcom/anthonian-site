About Our Congregation

The Congregation of the Sons of St. Anthony of Padua (Anthonian Missionaries) was founded on March 25, 2019, the Solemnity of the Annunciation of the Lord, by Very Rev. Fr. Dr. Constant Leke, a priest from the Catholic Diocese of Mamfe, Cameroon.

Despite being founded in Cameroon, the congregation has flourished dynamically across southern and southeastern Nigeria, with major footprints in Abia, Imo, and Cross River States. Our community is structured for both Catholic brotherhood and priesthood, dedicated to a life of prayer, study, and active charity.

Our Founder

Very Rev. Fr. Dr. Constant Leke envisioned a community of consecrated men who would combine rigorous intellectual formation with radical charity. His vision has grown into a vibrant missionary family spreading the Gospel through action and compassion.
