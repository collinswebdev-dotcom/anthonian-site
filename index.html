Welcome to the Anthonian Missionaries

The Congregation of the Sons of St. Anthony of Padua is a young, vibrant, diocesan-approved religious community dedicated to the care of the blind and visually impaired, rural evangelization, and the formation of holy priests and brothers.

Inspired by the humility and missionary zeal of our patron, St. Anthony of Padua, we strive to make the love of Christ visible through action, education, and compassionate service.

Our Motto

"Caritas Christi Urget Nos" — The Love of Christ Compels Us

And as St. Anthony taught: "Actions speak louder than words; let your words teach and your actions speak."

Quick Facts

  • Founded: March 25, 2019 (Solemnity of the Annunciation)
  • Founder: Very Rev. Fr. Dr. Constant Leke
  • Superior General: Rev. AnthonyMaryClaret Chisom Onyewuchi, CSAP
  • Presence: Nigeria, Cameroon & Philippines
  • Primary Apostolate: Care for the blind & visually impaired
