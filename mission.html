Our Mission

Core Apostolate: The primary mission of the Anthonian Missionaries is the care and support for the blind and visually impaired, alongside rural evangelization.

We are committed to imitating the humility, charity, and missionary zeal of St. Anthony of Padua, bringing the love of Christ to the most marginalized.

Our Charism

Our spirituality is anchored on St. Anthony of Padua — his humility, his evangelical preaching, and his profound love for the poor. We embrace his theology: "Actions speak louder than words; let your words teach and your actions speak."

Daily life centers around Holy Mass, scriptural meditation, and the manifestation of the "spirit of humility," which we view as the root of grace.

Our Apostolic Works

  • Specialized Care: Practical, spiritual, and physical aid to those suffering from blindness
  • Education: Infant Jesus Academy (Ekwerazu), Madonna Comprehensive College (Ahiazu Mbaise), Emmanuel Comprehensive College (Umulolo), Madonna Missionary Academy (Nguru)
  • Agricultural Self-Sufficiency: Poultry farms, goat rearing, crop plantations to support seminarians and communities
  • Rural Evangelization: Spreading the Gospel in underserved areas

Formation for Mission

The congregation trains its candidates at the Anthonian Institute of Philosophy (AIP) in Obubra, Cross River State, Nigeria. We also run the Anthonian Institute of Philosophy for intellectual and spiritual formation.
