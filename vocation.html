Discerning Your Vocation

The Anthonian Missionaries welcome young men who feel called to serve God as priests or brothers in the Catholic Church. We regularly host "Come and See" discernment programs.

📅 Upcoming Come & See Events

Admission Requirements

  • Practicing Catholic with a strong prayer life
  • O'Level SSCE certificate with credits in English and Christian Religious Studies (CRS)
  • Age Limit (O'Level holders): 18–25 years
  • Age Limit (University graduates): Up to 28 years
  • Good health and moral character

Formation Stages

  • Postulancy: Initial discernment and introduction to religious life
  • Novitiate: Intensive year of spiritual formation and study of vows
  • Philosophy & Theology: Academic formation at Anthonian Institute of Philosophy (AIP), Obubra
  • Pastoral Experience: Hands-on mission work in schools, parishes, and charitable apostolates
  • Perpetual Profession & Ordination: Final commitment and priesthood for those called

Formation Houses & Schools

  • Anthonian Institute of Philosophy (AIP), Obubra, Cross River State
  • Infant Jesus Academy, Ekwerazu
  • Madonna Comprehensive College, Ahiazu Mbaise
  • Emmanuel Comprehensive College, Umulolo
  • Madonna Missionary Academy, Nguru
